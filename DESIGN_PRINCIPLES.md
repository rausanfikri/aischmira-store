Photography is the Product

Typography is Branding

Consistency over Creativity

## Typography

Large headings.

Short paragraphs.

Editorial rhythm.

Comfortable line-height.

Never use excessive font weights.

---

## Layout

Centered containers.

Large whitespace.

Perfect alignment.

Symmetrical composition.

Premium grid.

---

## Motion

Subtle.

Elegant.

Purposeful.

No unnecessary animation.

Respect reduced motion.

---

## Imagery

Large images.

Minimal cropping.

Editorial composition.

Soft shadows.

Natural lighting.

---

## Buttons

Rounded.

Generous padding.

Elegant hover.

Smooth transition.

Accessible.

---

## Product Cards

Large images.

Minimal text.

Luxury spacing.

Consistent hierarchy.

---

## Footer

Balanced columns.

Centered logo.

Elegant social section.

Readable links.

Premium spacing.
