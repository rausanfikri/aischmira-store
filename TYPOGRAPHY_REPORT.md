## 1. Typography System Overview

The AISCHMIRA typography system combines **Cormorant Garamond** for editorial headings and **Inter** for UI, navigation, and body copy.

## 3. Verification

- Heading hierarchy audited with `h1` strictly limited to 1 per page.
- Line heights standardized to `1.2` for headings and `1.8` for body text.
