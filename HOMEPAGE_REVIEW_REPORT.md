## 1. Executive Summary

The AISCHMIRA Homepage has been completely redesigned into a luxury fashion flagship digital experience inspired by Patris, Loro Piana, Toteme, COS, Ralph Lauren, and Jacquemus.

Marketplace layout patterns, flash sale banners, and discount stickers were strictly excluded in favor of generous whitespace, editorial typography, subtle motion animations, and direct WhatsApp concierge commerce integration.
