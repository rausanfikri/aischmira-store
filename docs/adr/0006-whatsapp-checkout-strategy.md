# Title: WhatsApp Concierge Commerce as Purchase Channel
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-07-29
Context: AISCHMIRA is a luxury fashion flagship brand focused on high-touch client relationships, custom sizing assistance, and personal styling advice — not a commodity discount marketplace.
Decision: Shopping Bag checkout directs customers to a dedicated WhatsApp Concierge sales channel with structured order payloads.
Alternatives Considered: Standard e-commerce checkout (Stripe/Shopify-like). This would commoditize the brand experience.
Consequences: Eliminates complex checkout drop-offs, increases customer order value, and reinforces luxury brand positioning.
Future Review: Re-evaluate in Phase 4 when automated payment gateways might be introduced for VIP members.
