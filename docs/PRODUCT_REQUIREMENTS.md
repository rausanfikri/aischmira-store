# Product Requirements

## Product Vision
To be the digital flagship for AISCHMIRA, offering an unparalleled luxury fashion shopping experience that bridges online discovery with personalized concierge service.

## Business Goals
1. Establish a premium digital presence that elevates the brand.
2. Streamline the transition from product discovery to WhatsApp consultation.
3. Build a scalable foundation for a future loyalty and membership program.
4. Integrate seamlessly with existing inventory management (BigSeller).

## User Personas
1. **The Brand Loyal**: Returning customer, values personalized service, expects a seamless experience and recognition of their loyalty status.
2. **The Discovery Shopper**: First-time visitor, drawn by aesthetic and storytelling, needs clear product details and an easy path to inquire.
3. **The VIP Client**: High-net-worth individual, requires bespoke sizing and styling advice via the WhatsApp concierge.

## Customer Journey
1. **Discovery**: Lands on homepage or editorial collection page.
2. **Exploration**: Browses minimalist product grids, views high-resolution imagery.
3. **Consideration**: Views detailed product page with sizing, material, and care instructions.
4. **Action**: Adds to Shopping Bag.
5. **Checkout**: Proceeds to WhatsApp Concierge with a pre-filled order payload.

## Feature Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Current)**: Static Prototype & UI/UX Foundation.
- **Phase 2 (Sprint F2)**: CMS-Ready Architecture & Domain Restructuring.
- **Phase 3**: Supabase Database Integration (Dynamic Data).
- **Phase 4**: BigSeller Inventory Synchronization.
- **Phase 5**: Member & Loyalty Ecosystem.

## Functional Requirements
- Product Catalog displaying Collections and Categories.
- Shopping Bag to accumulate items for inquiry.
- WhatsApp redirect with structured order data.
- (Future) Member registration and login.
- (Future) Wishlist management.
- (Future) Loyalty points tracking.

## Non-functional Requirements
- **Performance**: Sub 1.5s LCP (Largest Contentful Paint).
- **Reliability**: 99.9% uptime (Vercel deployment).
- **Scalability**: Architecture must support 10x current traffic without major rewrites.

## Acceptance Criteria
Features are accepted when they meet the Definition of Done in the Project Constitution.

## Future Expansion
- Automated payment gateways (for specific user segments).
- Multi-currency and multi-language support.
- Advanced personalized recommendations.
